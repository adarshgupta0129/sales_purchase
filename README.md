This is a powerful Invoice Making and Billing Software designed to simplify financial transactions and streamline the billing process for businesses. It provides a complete solution for creating, managing, and storing invoices with all necessary calculations automatically handled by the system.

Key Features:
***Automated Calculations: Automatically calculates totals, taxes (GST/VAT), discounts, and final payable amounts, reducing human errors.

***Customizable Templates: Allows users to create professional invoices with customizable templates including company logo, address, and customer details.

***Offline Functionality: The software works completely offline, meaning there is no need for an internet connection. All operations can be performed locally on your system.

***User-Friendly Interface: Simple and intuitive design makes it easy for users to generate invoices quickly without any technical knowledge.

***Data Storage: All data is stored locally, ensuring privacy and giving users full control over their records.

***Print and Export Options: Invoices can be printed or exported as PDF for sharing and record-keeping.

This software is ideal for small to medium-sized businesses, freelancers, and shop owners who need a reliable and efficient tool to manage their billing without depending on the internet.
